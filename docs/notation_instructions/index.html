Performance Instructions

Bloom — Convergence — Balance, for Tuba Ensemble — Justin Yang

The score for Bloom — Convergence — Balance is a computer-animated score, served from the cloud and played in a web browser, like an online video game.

Animated Conduction Tools

There are a number of animated conduction tools that are intuitive to follow and aid performers in realizing the score.

A scrolling cursor runs throughout the piece at a consistent speed and indicates when things are to be played. The notation is proportional: horizontal space corresponds directly to time. Bouncing-ball conductors aid with rhythmic precision. The dotted vertical line marks the go-time.

In the first example, play the notated staccato A when the scrolling cursor reaches the go line and the ball bounces. In the second example, begin the G at go-time and hold until the cursor reaches the end of the line.

Curve-Based Crescendo

Play the crescendo from dynamic 1 to dynamic 2. The bottom of the curve corresponds to the starting dynamic, the top to the ending dynamic. The contour of the curve determines how the crescendo grows over time.

A curve follower gives your real-time position in the curve.

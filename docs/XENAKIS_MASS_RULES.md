# Xenakis mass rules — boiled from Metastaseis / Pithoprakta scholarship

*Research pass 2026-08-10 (sources at bottom). The composer's redirect: study how the
acoustic sound-masses were actually built; granular metaphor on hold.*

## The central discovery: TWO distinct mass species

**Point-cloud (Pithoprakta, bars 52–59).** 46 independent string parts; **every
event is a pizzicato — a sharp attack — triggering a short glissando tail.** Only ONE
parameter is stochastic (gliss speed, Maxwell-Boltzmann distributed). Density = the
ensemble onset rate, legible because the atoms are countable attacks arriving at
tens per second.

**Line-mass (Metastaseis, opening).** 61 soloists, zero doubling; a unison G
**splinters into 46 sustained glissandi** — ruled-surface geometry projected onto
pitch × time. After the entries there are NO new onsets: the density profile lives
in **pitch-space divergence** and dynamics, not event rate.

Both produce unmistakable growth/shrink profiles — through opposite means.

## The rules

- **X1 — Choose the species per passage.** Point-cloud or line-mass; an accidental
  hybrid (onset-less points, spaceless lines) carries neither cue.
- **X2 — Atoms are simple; complexity lives only in the aggregate.** A pizz, a stroke,
  a plain tone. (Our breath-swell grain is already a compound phrase — too much
  interior event to be an atom.)
- **X3 — Attack salience.** Onset density is only countable when onsets are sharp.
  **Fades cannot carry onset-density motion** — the direct cause of finding 13:
  strat-001 was a cloud of soft-attacked swells, i.e. onset-less points on one pitch —
  neither species.
- **X4 — Total individuation.** Everyone a soloist, no doubling. (Already our practice.)
- **X5 — Stochastic restraint.** Bars 52–59 randomize ONE parameter; everything else
  is plain. Scatter what matters, fix the rest.
- **X6 — Rates.** Cloud legibility at tens of onsets/s ensemble-wide. Tongued tuba
  points at 2–4/s per player → 7 tubas reach **15–25 onsets/s** — genuine
  point-cloud territory. Breath-swells cap near 3/s → swell textures are TEXTURE,
  not cloud-motion, and shouldn't be asked to carry it.
- **X7 — Pitch space is the other density axis.** Metastaseis grows by divergence
  from unison. Our unison confinement removed this axis entirely; even a small
  spread reopens it. (Resurfaces the S6 material question.)
- **X8 — Terraced evolution.** The graph-cell construction moves density in steps —
  discrete states the ear can compare — not only smooth ramps.

## What this means for the seven tubas

- **The tuba point-cloud is feasible**: short tongued notes (~0.15–0.35 s, sharp
  attack, expodec-shaped) at terraced ensemble rates 2 → 6 → 14 → 20/s. Proposed
  test XT1 (unison first; pitch spread as the follow-up per X7).
- **The tuba line-mass** wants glissandi spreading from unison — 7 lines vs
  Xenakis's 46, but pitch-spread + dynamics may still read. Requires opening pitch
  (composer's call). Proposed test XT2.
- **The breath-swell mass remains OURS** — a third species Xenakis didn't have
  (his atoms couldn't breathe). Keep it as texture/color; stop asking it to carry
  density-motion (finding 13 closed).

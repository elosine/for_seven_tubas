# Penn State application research — tuba/euphonium ensemble size

*Researched 2026-08-10 for the Penn State International New-Music Festival &
Symposium application (tuba/euphonium ensemble category, minimum 6 parts).*

## Festival facts

- **4th Biennial International New-Music Festival & Symposium: March 18–20, 2027.**
- **Submission deadline: Friday, September 4, 2026, 11:59 pm EST** — ~3.5 weeks out.
- Up to two works per composer; 31 categories; selected composers attend in person
  and work with the performers.
- Call page: https://sites.psu.edu/pennstatecomposition/festival-and-call-for-scores/

## Penn State's ensemble

- The Tuba Euphonium Ensemble is directed by **Velvet Brown** (Distinguished
  Professor, David P. Stone Chair) — one of the country's premier tuba/euphonium
  studios; ensemble formed 1987; repeated ITEC appearances (1995–2014+); annual
  Octubafest/TUBAween concerts.
- **Best direct evidence of scale:** the 2024-ish TUBAween program included "a
  Beatles medley featuring NINE euphonium players" — euphoniums alone ≥ 9, implying
  a full studio (tubas + euphs) plausibly in the mid-to-high teens.
- No published roster count found on the site.

## Comparable university ensembles

- **Southern Miss**: studio averages **16–20 students**; tuba/euphonium choir
  currently **21 players**.
- Big-program norm (North Texas, Tennessee, Indiana, Tennessee Tech): studio-wide
  choirs typically **10–20+**.

## Estimate & recommendation

- **Ballpark for Penn State: ~12–20 players available; ≥ 9 euphoniums documented.**
  A 10-part piece is comfortably within reach; even doubled parts are plausible.
- **Our 10-part + M1 design is ideal for this call**: 10 base parts satisfies
  "minimum 6" with headroom, and the on-the-fly part-multiplication mandate (M1)
  means the piece scales to WHATEVER roster shows up (e.g., 19 independent parts
  from the 10) — a genuine selling point to mention in the application.
- **Verification path** (composer, if wanted): count players in the videos —
  [Octubafest Tuba Euphonium Ensemble Concert](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WEXXzeIixH4) ·
  [Penn State Tuba Euphonium Ensemble Virtual Concert](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X8CNLNwBSNA) ·
  [Octubafest #4 — Tuba/Euph Ensemble & Trombone Choir](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BrVVWrx58PA)
